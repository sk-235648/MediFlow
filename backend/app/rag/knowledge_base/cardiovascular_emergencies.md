# Cardiovascular Emergencies & Acute Coronary Protocol

## 1. Acute Coronary Syndromes (ACS) / Myocardial Infarction
### Clinical Presentation & Red Flags
- Retrosternal chest pressure, heaviness, tightness, squeezing, or burning sensation.
- Radiation to left arm, both arms, shoulder, neck, jaw, or epigastrium.
- Associated vegetative symptoms: Diaphoresis (cold clammy sweat), acute dyspnea, nausea, lightheadedness, or presyncope.
- Atypical presentations: Women, diabetics, and elderly patients frequently present without classic crushing chest pain, presenting instead with isolated dyspnea, sudden fatigue, vomiting, or epigastric discomfort.
- **Triage Level**: **ESI Level 2** (Emergent).
- **Timeframe**: Immediate ECG within 10 minutes of arrival; call Emergency Medical Services (911 / EMS) immediately; do not drive oneself.

## 2. Aortic Dissection
### Clinical Presentation & Red Flags
- Abrupt onset of tearing, ripping, or knife-like chest or upper back pain (often between scapulae).
- Pain is maximal at the exact onset (unlike gradual crescendo of myocardial ischemia).
- Pulse deficit (difference in radial or femoral pulses between extremities) or systolic BP differential > 20 mmHg between arms.
- Associated focal neurologic deficit or acute aortic valve insufficiency murmur.
- **Triage Level**: **ESI Level 1 or 2**.
- **Action**: Immediate trauma/resuscitation room, urgent CT aortography.

## 3. Hypertensive Crisis (Emergency vs. Urgency)
- Hypertensive Emergency: Blood pressure > 180/120 mmHg WITH acute target organ damage (encephalopathy, chest pain, stroke signs, acute renal failure, pulmonary edema, eclampsia). **ESI Level 2**.
- Hypertensive Urgency: Blood pressure > 180/120 mmHg WITHOUT acute target organ damage. Requires outpatient adjustment or urgent primary care evaluation within 24-48 hours. **ESI Level 4 or 3**.

## 4. Cardiac Arrhythmias & Syncope
- Unstable Arrhythmia: Heart rate < 40 bpm or > 150 bpm with hypotension, chest pain, altered consciousness, or shock. **ESI Level 1**.
- Stable Tachycardia / Palpitations: Normal blood pressure, no chest pain or syncope, requires ECG and basic lab work. **ESI Level 3**.
