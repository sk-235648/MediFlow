# Emergency Severity Index (ESI) Triage Handbook (v4/v5)

## Overview
The Emergency Severity Index (ESI) is a 5-level triage algorithm that stratifies patients based on patient acuity and anticipated resource needs.

```
                  [Does this patient require immediate life-saving intervention?]
                                 /                                \
                              Yes                                  No
                              /                                      \
                           Level 1                       [Is this a high-risk situation?
                        (Resuscitation)                   Or confused/lethargic/disoriented?
                                                          Or severe pain/distress?]
                                                                 /            \
                                                              Yes              No
                                                              /                  \
                                                           Level 2          [How many resources
                                                          (Emergent)         are anticipated?]
                                                                               /     |     \
                                                                            None    One    Many (2+)
                                                                            /        |       \
                                                                        Level 5   Level 4   [Danger Zone
                                                                        (Non-     (Less      Vitals?]
                                                                        urgent)   urgent)    /      \
                                                                                           Yes       No
                                                                                           /           \
                                                                                       Level 2       Level 3
                                                                                       (Emergent)   (Urgent)
```

## ESI Decision Algorithm

### Decision Point A: Immediate Life-Saving Intervention Required? (ESI Level 1)
- Airway: Unprotected or obstructed airway, agonal respirations, apnea.
- Breathing: Severe respiratory arrest or pre-arrest.
- Circulation: Pulselessness, severe bradycardia or tachycardia with hemodynamic collapse, uncontrolled exsanguinating hemorrhage.
- Mental Status: Completely unresponsive (AVPU scale = "U").
- **Action**: Immediate resuscitation bay, doctor immediately at bedside. Care window: 0 minutes.

### Decision Point B: High-Risk Situation / Severe Pain / Altered Mentation? (ESI Level 2)
- High Risk Presentation:
  - Suspected Acute Coronary Syndrome (chest pressure, diaphoresis, radiation to jaw/arm).
  - Suspected Acute Stroke (BE-FAST signs within thrombolysis/thrombectomy window).
  - Severe respiratory distress with accessory muscle use or stridor.
  - Anaphylaxis with airway/lip swelling or wheezing.
  - Ectopic pregnancy risk in reproductive female with acute pelvic pain and syncope.
  - Suicidal or homicidal ideation with active intent or weapon.
- Severe pain or distress (Pain score 7-10/10 with physiological distress).
- Lethargic, confused, or acutely disoriented patient.
- **Action**: Placement within 10-15 minutes in monitored bed.

### Decision Point C: Anticipated Resource Consumption (ESI Levels 3, 4, 5)
- **What constitutes an ESI Resource?**
  - Included: Labs (blood, urine), ECG, X-rays/CT/MRI/Ultrasound, IV fluids for hydration, IV/IM medications, specialty consultation, complex procedure (e.g. laceration repair, chest tube).
  - Excluded (NOT resources): History & physical exam, point-of-care fingerstick glucose, simple splint/sling, tetanus booster, prescription writing, crutches.
- **Resource Stratification**:
  - **No resources anticipated**: **ESI Level 5** (e.g. suture removal, medication refill, mild viral URI rash).
  - **One resource anticipated**: **ESI Level 4** (e.g. simple ankle X-ray, urine dipstick + antibiotics for uncomplicated cystitis, single simple suture).
  - **Two or more resources anticipated**: **ESI Level 3** (e.g. abdominal pain requiring labs + CT + IV analgesia).

### Decision Point D: Danger Zone Vital Signs (Upgrade to ESI Level 2)
If a patient would otherwise be Level 3, check systemic vitals:
- Heart Rate: > 100 bpm (tachycardia) or < 50 bpm (severe bradycardia).
- Respiratory Rate: > 20 bpm (tachypnea) or < 10 bpm.
- Oxygen Saturation (SpO2): < 92% on room air.
- Temperature: > 38.5°C (101.3°F) with systemic toxicity or immunocompromised state.
- If vitals exceed danger criteria, the triage clinician MUST upgrade the patient to **ESI Level 2**.
